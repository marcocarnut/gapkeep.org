Logo concepts

Four directions for GapKeep's logo, each a mark and a wordmark (the letters are paths: the files need no font). All work in one colour and at favicon size. Colours are GapKeep's own: teal #00968c, amber #ffc300, on #1d1c23. They are made by tools/make_logos.py; the one in use is its USE.

Finder

A QR code's finder pattern read as a keep: a wall, a gap all around, and the secret kept in the middle. QR codes are how data crosses GapKeep's air gap.

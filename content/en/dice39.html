dice39

The dice tables

dice39 turns dice and a coin into the words of a BIP-39 seed phrase: the coin picks the table, and two dice give a row and a column. These are the tables to print, with a sheet to note your rolls on. They hold no secret: everyone's are the same.

  1. Download

    • English: A4 · Letter
    • Português (Brasil): A4 · Carta

    Six landscape pages: Table A (heads) and Table B (tails) on two pages each, the sheet for your rolls, and what to do when the last word is in. The BIP-39 words are English in both; only the instructions differ.

  2. Print

    At actual size (100%), landscape. Use a printer that doesn't keep a copy of what it prints: the tables are no secret, but the sheet you'll write on soon will be.

  3. Roll

    Open dice39 on GapKeep and choose Enter my dice words; the app says how to read the dice. Type up to 22 words (at 24) and the device adds the rest, or all of them, to split a phrase made only of your throws. The sheet with your rolls is a copy of your seed in the making: once the phrase is safely written down or split, destroy it (shred it, or burn it). Never photograph it.

These are dice39's own tables, printed from its web page (bip39.postcogito.org), which builds them from the same wordlist the app checks words against; you can print them from there on any computer too.
